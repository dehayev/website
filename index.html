Editing Methodology

THE CREATIVE WORKFLOW

01

Ingest & Story Curation

Organizing multi-cam footage, logging audio takes, and selecting the most compelling moments to build a solid narrative timeline.

02

Color Grading & Film Look

Applying color space transforms, balancing skin tones, matching shots across different cameras, and crafting filmic color grades.

03

Motion & VFX Polish

Enhancing motion with smooth speed ramps, optical flow frame rate interpolation, title graphics, and seamless visual cleanups.

04

Audio Master & Delivery

Fine-tuning dialogue clarity, sound effects, music dynamics, and exporting high-bitrate master files tailored for web or broadcast.
